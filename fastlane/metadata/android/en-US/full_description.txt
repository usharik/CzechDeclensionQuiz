Learn Czech grammar by doing it. Czech Declension Quiz drills the forms that make Czech hard: the seven cases in singular and plural, noun and adjective declension, adjective agreement and verb conjugation.

Three word classes, one app
• Nouns: 912 words with gender, declension pattern and translation
• Adjectives: 506 words, always practiced together with a noun so you learn agreement (mladý muž → mladého muže, jarní noc → jarní noci)
• Verbs: 581 words with the present tense, past participle and imperative, plus aspect pairs (dělat / udělat)
• Phrases: decline an adjective and a noun together after bez, ke, vidím, o, s

Two ways to practice
• Full table: drag every form into its place in the declension or conjugation table
• One form at a time: pick the right form, case by case or person by person

Learn from your mistakes
Every word you get wrong goes to your mistakes list. Review it until it's empty.

Grammar handbook
Declension patterns for nouns (pán, hrad, žena, město and others), hard, soft and possessive adjectives, and the main verb groups, with short grammar tips: stem softening, the imperative, the past tense with jsem / jsi.

Stay motivated
• Daily goal with points and a streak
• Optional daily reminder
• Filter nouns by gender
